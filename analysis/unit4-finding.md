# Leadership finding: Recorded no-shows are concentrated in a few producers

The platform's recorded passenger no-show rate is **6.00%: 12 of 200 bookings** in the supplied 2023 dataset. This uses all bookings as the denominator. There are also 22 other cancellations and 10 disputes; those are separate outcomes, not additional recorded no-shows.

Producer rates range from **0% to 18.75%** among the 13 producers with bookings. Four producers have no recorded no-shows. Two other producers have no bookings, so their rates cannot be assessed. The eight producers with at least 15 bookings have a combined rate of **4.83%** (7 of 145). This volume screen helps prioritize review, but the sample remains small.

I would investigate these two producers first:

| Producer ID | Bookings | Recorded no-shows | Rate | Share of platform no-shows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 16 | 3 | 18.75% | 25.00% |
| 3 | 13 | 2 | 15.38% | 16.67% |

**Producer 2 is the first priority:** it has the largest no-show count and the highest rate, and it meets the volume screen. **Producer 3 merits an exploratory review:** it has the second-largest count but falls below that screen. Together, these two account for 5 of 12 no-shows (41.67%) from 29 of 200 bookings (14.5%). Producer 4 also has a high rate, 16.67%, but that represents just one no-show in six bookings; I would monitor it and collect more observations before escalating it.

By booking market, Thailand has the most no-shows, 4 of 40 bookings (10%). Korea and Vietnam each have 3; Japan and Singapore each have 1. These are booking-country groups, which may differ from flight origins. They suggest where regional staff can help review records, without establishing that a market or producer caused the outcome.

The Director of Airline Operations should review the underlying cases for producers 2 and 3, check repeat-passenger patterns and recording accuracy, and compare monthly and market-adjusted results before taking corrective action. A recorded passenger no-show is not proof of producer fault. These findings support investigation, not penalties or a new payout hold.
